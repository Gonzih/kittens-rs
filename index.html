Our flagship

A coding agent with nine lives.

Agent loops juggle prompts, tools, streaming, cancellation, budgets, and memory. kittens-code keeps the run visible and recoverable—so a crash is an interruption, not amnesia.

01

Keep the thread.

Your session history survives restarts and replays cleanly when work resumes.

02

Recall what matters.

03

Make failure boring.

Bounded work and predictable recovery turn nasty async edges into testable behavior.
